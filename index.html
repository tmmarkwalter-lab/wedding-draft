Good to know

Questions

What should I wear?

Formal attire, for an elegant evening in the mountains. The ceremony and reception are on grass, so skip the stilettos.

What will the weather be like?

Mid-September in Whitefish is usually crisp and sunny during the day and cool once the sun sets. Bring a wrap or jacket for the evening.

How do I get to the ranch?

Shuttles run from set pickup spots in Whitefish to the ranch and back, on a schedule. We'll post the full timetable here closer to the weekend.

What else should we do while we're there?

Drive Going-to-the-Sun Road in Glacier National Park, paddle Whitefish Lake, ride the gondola on Whitefish Mountain, wander downtown Whitefish, and ask Thomas where the fish are biting.
